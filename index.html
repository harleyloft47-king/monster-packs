How to play

Rip open packs of monsters from the world's myths and legends, and collect them all.

Open Monster Packs
  • Five packs: Man-Eaters, Witches, Ghouls, Cursed Beasts and Spirits. Drag the ring (or use the arrows) to spin a pack to the front.
  • Every pack holds 5 cards. Tap Open, then drag your finger across the top of the pack to tear it. The strip peels as far as you tear.
  • Tap or swipe to flip through your cards one at a time. The last card is always Rare or better.
  • Three rarities: Common ◆ (silver), Rare ◆◆ (gold) and Legendary ◆◆◆ (rainbow).
  • Legendaries are the rarest of all, but you're guaranteed at least one every 30 packs.
  • Very rarely you'll open a Blood Moon Pack: every card Rare or better, with at least one Legendary.
Free packs & coins
  • You get a free pack every 12 hours, and up to 2 can wait for you.
  • Extra packs cost 1,000 coins. Buy them into My Stash and open them whenever you like.
  • Earn coins from badges and by selling duplicates: Common 25, Rare 150, Legendary 1,500.
  • Need more? Tap ＋ Get coins under your coin count. The shop is in test mode for now, so no real money is charged.
Saving
  • Your collection is saved in this browser on this device.
